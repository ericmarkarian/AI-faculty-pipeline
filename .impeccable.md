# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Faculty recruiters, department chairs, research administrators, and academic search committees at research universities. Used during working hours at a desk — deliberate, task-focused sessions where they are evaluating real candidates for real positions. The stakes are high: these decisions shape department composition and careers. Users are highly educated and expect the interface to match their standards.

### Brand Personality
**3 words: deliberate · institutional · discerning**

This is not a consumer product or a startup tool — it is closer in spirit to an executive search firm's research database or a prestigious academic registry. The emotional goal is quiet confidence: users should feel they are working with something authoritative and careful, not something that was generated or templated. The hiring-esque quality means human careers are at the center of every data row.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Light mode.** Refined and editorial without being stuffy. Think the visual weight of a university press publication, a faculty appointment letter, or a well-typeset academic journal — not a hospital EMR or a generic SaaS dashboard.

**Accent color: deep forest green** (`oklch(38% 0.12 160)`). Avoids the institutional-blue monoculture and the Stanford-red legacy. Forest green reads as cultivated, serious, and unusual in this category — it signals discernment without loudness.

**Fonts:**
- Display/headings: **Bodoni Moda** — high-contrast editorial serif, evokes quality print publications, faculty appointment letters. Used for the application name, prominent labels, and faculty names.
- UI/body: **Manrope** — precise geometric-humanist sans-serif, excellent at small data sizes, slightly more exacting than humanist alternatives. Used for all data, labels, filters, and body copy.

**Anti-references:**
- Generic AI-generated UI (gradient cards, glassmorphism, cyan-on-dark)
- Hospital EMR / clinical software (utilitarian grey, color-coded status rows, dense form grids)
- Consumer fintech / startup dashboards (metrics everywhere, rounded cards, motivational copy)
- Anything that looks like it was built from a UI kit without adaptation

### Design Principles

1. **Typography is the hierarchy.** Size, weight, and spacing carry meaning — not color or decoration. The type scale has real contrast between steps (at least 1.25× ratio).

2. **Data is the product.** Faculty information needs to breathe. Avoid wrapping rows in cards, nesting containers, or burying data under chrome. The table/list IS the interface — honor it.

3. **Credibility through restraint.** Nothing decorative without purpose. An interface that earns trust by being precise is more powerful than one that earns attention by being busy. Every element must earn its place.

4. **Institutional without dull.** The forest green accent, quality typography, and careful spatial rhythm distinguish this from generic admin tools. Interesting ≠ loud. Professionalism ≠ beige.

5. **People, not rows.** Faculty are individuals whose careers this tool affects. Names are anchors — visually prominent. Metrics (NIH grants, h-index, roles) are supporting context, not the star.

### Technical Constraints
- React via CDN + Babel (no build step) — single HTML file, inline styles
- No external CSS framework
- Light mode only
- Desktop-first (no mobile requirement)
- Data is loaded from `faculty_output.json` — mostly tabular with nested arrays for roles
